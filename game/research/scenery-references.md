# Scenery references: lifts and buildings

Collected 2026-11-25 for the Blender scenery models (`tools/blender/build_lifts.py`, `build_buildings.py`). Text sources (remontees-mecaniques.net reports, Liftblog captions) give specs but almost no colours or shapes; the descriptions below come from looking at the photos. Specs are in `apex-references.md` §3.3.

## Quickdraw (Doppelmayr-CTEC detachable quad, 1994)

Photos: Liftblog, Feb 2018, `https://liftblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/img_NNNN.jpg`.

- Bottom return (`img_1584`, `img_1586`, `img_1587`): an early Uni terminal. A long, low housing with rounded ends: white lower band, a sloped band of dark tinted glass all round, flat grey roof panels. Two square grey columns, a service ladder, a curved grey chair-parking rail off the side, a blue Doppelmayr plate on the underside. Beside it: a small white flat-roofed operator hut with windows, a blue "Quickdraw" safety board, a yellow "wait for lift operator" sign, red poles with orange rope fencing, and a tall thin antenna mast.
- Top drive (`img_1606`, `img_1661`, `img_1663`): the same rounded housing and glass band with a dark corrugated hipped roof over it, one large square column and a rear leg, a grey timber unload deck with railings and stairs, a grey-sided operator hut with a snowy gable roof.
- Towers (`img_1595`, `img_1616`): single round grey tubes with a yellow-orange foam pad at the base, a T crossarm with a railed walkway and sheave trains. RM.net lists the trains per tower (P1 8C/8C, mostly 6S/4S, P14 to P18 8S/6S).
- Chairs (`img_1587`, `img_1595`): open grey galvanized quads, a dark slatted bench and low back, a curved hanger, a safety bar with footrest. No padding, no bubble.

## Stock's (GMD Müller fixed-grip triple, 1981)

- Chairs and towers (`img_1632`): grey triple benches with a tall loop-shaped back frame; grey tube towers with yellow base pads, a short crossarm and a small lifting frame on top.
- Top drive (`img_1642`): a tall A-frame cabin with dark stained vertical boards, two windows and a small upper window, a steep dark metal roof shedding snow, standing on splayed steel legs over a horizontal bullwheel. Diesel drive (no grid power). Fire damage in 2014 or 2017, repaired.
- Bottom return (`img_1658`): a green steel portal with splayed legs, a horizontal bullwheel beam with sheaves, a green counterweight box; a small white gable hut behind.

## T-Bar (GMD Müller, 1968)

- Base (`img_1577`, `img_1675`): a tall blue cylindrical counterweight column painted with white snowflakes, a shorter blue drive column topped by a yellow horizontal bullwheel, a grey operator building.
- Towers (`img_1574`, `img_1671`): yellow lattice portal gantries.
- Top (`img_1689`, `img_1690`): a yellow lattice portal carrying the return wheel, a small dark timber lookout hut with a big window, and a run board (blue panels, white lettering: Rookies Trail, Okanagan Run, Spruce Hollow, Terrain Parks).

## Gunbarrel Saloon

Photos: the saloon's own site `thegunbarrelsaloon.ca` (via image search) and a night village photo.

- A long, low timber building facing the slope, one to two storeys: warm brown board-and-batten walls, two prominent front gables with exposed log trusses and tall glass, broad shallow roofs with deep snow, a snow-loaded shed-roof porch over the entrance.
- A long deck at snow level along the slope side with horizontal log rails and posts, picnic tables, crowds at après.
- String lights along the eaves; a hanging round wooden sign ("GUNBARREL" in arched letters with a revolver and a beer mug; the logo also uses a moose head); a Canadian flag.
- A taller hotel block adjoins one end. Inside (not modelled): a gutted snowcat as the DJ booth, vintage Apex memorabilia, a rock fireplace. It once had rooftop hot tubs.

Only a caption (sonnybou.ca, 2016): the summit hut, formerly a café selling cookies and hot drinks, now the ski patrol cabin. No photo detail was readable.
