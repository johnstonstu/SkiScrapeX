## Mountain-wide feel (highest impact)
1. Altitude tree biome: normal snowy dark conifers low down, rime-encased "snow ghosts" near the summit and ridges, spaced wider up top.
2. Atmosphere: mostly overcast diffuse light; fog at the summit that clears lower down; a valley CLOUD INVERSION plane seen from Ridge Run and the groomers, which can also hide valley geometry for performance.
3. Terrain character: "so much fall line." Black runs drop like fingers off the main ridge traverse, are sustained, and have no flats. Most blacks are tree-walled corridors with soft chop and moguls.
4. Snow surfaces: groomer corduroy; chopped soft snow and moguls on blacks; irregular traffic bumps under the lifts; thin rocky entrances (Tooth). Cold, soft interior snow.
5. Audio by surface: a zipper/carve sound plus wind on groomers, scrape and chop on blacks, rhythmic thuds in moguls, muffled swoosh in powder, rock scrape on thin cover; lift hum and sheave clicks; crunchy footsteps in the village.
6. Built world: a colourful painted-wood village (yellow, green, blue) with balconies, heavy roof snow and icicles; a gravel lot; a ticket booth; a small village park. The summit has a white/green lift shack, a lattice radio tower, orange slat fencing and "Cliff Area" signs. Lift towers have yellow pads close to run edges. Wooden trail signposts (Tooth/Tusk). A "BEST WAY DOWN" sign near the bottom of K2.
7. People and vibe: quiet runs, locals, a dancing liftie with a music speaker at the Quickdraw base.
