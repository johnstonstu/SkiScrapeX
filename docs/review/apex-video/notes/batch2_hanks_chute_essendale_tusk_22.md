# Batch 2: Hanks, Chute, Essendale, Tooth/Tusk, Twenty Two
- Hanks (black): steep, sustained fall line, medium soft moguls, a narrow corridor between tree walls, blue sky with high contrast.
- Chute (black): a gentle, near-flat approach curving slightly left, then a BLIND ROLLOVER, then a sustained steep straight fall line. A consistent hallway width (the model's estimate is about 30-40 ft, which is uncertain; LiDAR has the real width). Smooth and packed, no prominent moguls, so good for straight-lining. Strict tree walls, with a distant peak framed dead centre down the line. "Lots of little booters everywhere." The model says the start is beside a large timber lodge and a small hut. This is UNCERTAIN, since the run starts high on the mountain, so it may be the top terminal or a patrol hut. The clip ends before the run-out.
- Essendale (black): a flat traverse through tight snowy trees, then an open moderate bowl with scattered saplings. Misty valley. "This area is known as the bowls ... powder skiing straight in the sun."
- Tooth/Tusk (double black): a steep cornice/rollover drop-in funnelling into a tight choke with probable rock bands, deep shadows and check turns. A snow-covered wooden signpost points left to "Tooth" and right to "Tusk". The skiers launch "off that bottom point into the Tooth Tusk bowl."
- Twenty Two (black): steep, narrow and straight like Chute but bumpy, with long shadows across the run. The lap starts on the quad: "my head almost exploded."
