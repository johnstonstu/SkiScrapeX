# Apex video lap notes

Method: YouTube watch pages in the browser pane, 0.7-scale zooms of the player. About 70 zooms taken, about 55 readable (the first zoom after each navigation was usually grey; one video began with ads, not clicked). Auto-captions are unreliable, so identification relies on signs, lifts and landmarks. T values below are start-time plus approximate wait.

## 1. Apex NORAM, BC's HIDDEN GEM! (Daniel Tanner)
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Huoz3VzTIPo (year not shown; winter, blue sky). Watched t=0-30, 60-90, 120-150, 200-230. The video was NOT watched to the end.
- t~25: rider to camera in a mogul-team style bib, bluebird, rimed trees, mountain ridge behind (guess: NORAM training).
- t~75 (village view): a skier approaches the camera on a groomed slope; below, a yellow-and-brown lodge building and smaller cabins on the left, a small orange padded marker/box at the lip. Forested ridge across the valley. HOW: village-side buildings. Guess: this is the mogul run near the base, seen from the top of the course.
- t~80-90: the course proper. Corridor is lined by a blue fence on the skier's right (the viewer's right as the skier comes down). Red/orange rectangular padded markers sit inside the lane, on its edges. Spectators and officials stand at the top/left behind small red blocks. Behind: tall green trees with heavy snow cover, close to the course on both sides.
- t~95-100: a single skier in the middle of a mogul line, blue fence on the right, red markers on both sides. Bumps are medium, rounded, regular, with a soft white snow surface. Pitch is moderate, steeper than a groom. Width is roughly 3 to 4 skier-lengths for the lane, with a blue fence line right at the edge.
- t~120-150: the skier (in the lift line) talks to camera. "Day two of training": the skier avoided the right lines because they "drifted hard" (caption, partly garbled). Background: a lodge/cabins with a red-brown roof at the base. Lift cable overhead with chairs.
- t~150: the camera sits under a T-bar-like loading gate: two yellow steel A-frame pylons wrapped in yellow padding, a lift line cable between. HOW: yellow padded towers plus cable. Guess: T-bar loading/bottom or a tower near the bottom of the T-bar.
- t~205-230: a skier alone on a very wide white pitch (flat light), orange/red markers; a red-orange line of gate-like poles across the snow in front of fresh trees (t~220). Trees are green with heavy snow, standing right at the edge of the course within 2 to 3 skier-lengths. Bumps are low.

## 4. Grandmother's Trail, Apex Mountain Resort, BC (Matt Oviatt)
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9l3cnZh--7Q (date stamp 2021-04-27 or so). Watched t~35-90 after about 30 s of ads.
- t~35-75: the camera looks at a wooden sign post with a blue square sign at the start of the trail. HOW: the title says "Grandmother's Trail" on the Wild Side; sign text is not legible. Beyond: wide open slope falling away to the left, a wooden fence/stake line running along the lip on the left, sparse small green trees, and a big dark ridge with white upper peaks beyond. - t~85-90: the trail goes down; a loose-edged open face with rimed/shaded trees on the upper left, wind-scoured, sun-crust texture. Gentle to moderate.

## 6. Apex Mountain Resort | Winter Slopes and Mountain Views (Lifelong Trail)
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JQN6lDrrf-4 (26 min, winter 2025-ish). Chapters in the description include Quad Lift ride, scenic view from the top, Grandfathers Run descent, T-Bar, Hill of Moguls and Youth freestyle. This was the most useful video for the top of Quickdraw.
- t~105-110: summit view just off the Quickdraw (by the chapter title and the flow). Very broad, gentle open ridgetop with low sun-crust snow, wooden snow fence line along the left, rolling tundra-like terrain far away, no big trees. Flat to gentle pitch.
- t~115-125: the camera glides along a groomed track; deep snow-ghost spruce beside it on the right; a wooden slatted snow fence (about a metre high) runs along the left edge of the track and angles away. The groomed track is about 6 to 10 skier-widths wide and nearly flat.
- t~140-150: groomed track with fresh corduroy, a colored sign post with a red/yellow disc at the far left, some rimed spruce clusters, long views.
- t~150-165: the flat groomed ridge continues, snow-covered trees on the left, a radio mast/tower on a rise ahead and a small cabin or hut by the mast beyond. HOW: a lattice mast visible against the sky. This is the Grandfathers Trail traverse out from the top (guess based on the sign at t~180).
- t~180 SIGN (very useful): a wooden two-board post. Upper board: black diamond with an arrow pointing up-left, text "Chute". Lower board: green circle, arrow left, text "Grandfathers Trail", with smaller text below saying it goes to the village (small text, not fully legible). Spruce with snow behind.
- t~185-200: right after the sign, the skier drops left into untracked deep snow: a steep-feeling open pitch with small rimed trees at the lip and blue sky. Guess: Chute entrance area.
- t~245-265: Grandfathers Trail lower down: wide corduroy, tree lines on both sides (green and snow-laden spruce, about 4 to 6 skier-widths from the centreline), pitch gentle. Skiers in the distance for scale. The run is straight, fairly long, and gently sloped, with a view over distant hills.
- t~430-465: wide groomed blue/green run toward the village. A cabin building with a snow-covered roof appears at the left edge (t~445, guess: a lodge near the base). Spruce on the left, a roll on the right.
- t~1240: lift unload/load area with a snow igloo, a small snowman and an orange cone, a sign board, a chair on the right and steel pylons on the left. Guess: the top of the T-Bar or the top of the beginner chair; the trees beyond are tall green firs.
- t~1260-1270: forest edge of the run, green firs left, rimed on the right, slightly rolling.
- t~1440-1445: from inside a building (looking through a window): a children's area with a small groomed zone, red/orange kicker features at the bottom right, a lodge and a skier. The camera shows rope fences.
- t~1460-1470 (best wide shot): standing at the top of the mogul/aerial hill and looking down to the village: moguls on a short steep pitch in the foreground, a blue fence line along the bottom of the frame at lower left, small bamboo/stakes on the left edge, an evergreen forest on the right with a cat track/road running through; below, the valley bottom with the village/lodge cluster and parking and a cleared road. Facing across the valley: a long forested ridge with white clearings (ski runs on the opposite side). HOW: framing from the freestyle hill and village; the grouping is a guess.

## Summary by place

1. Top of Quickdraw and the Grandfathers traverse
 - Video 6 t=105-165: the area at the top is a wide open ridge, nearly flat, with a wooden slatted snow fence on the skier's left side, sparse snow-ghost spruce, and a groomed track 6 to 10 skier-widths wide. A lattice radio mast with a small hut is visible ahead on a rise, so it is at a distance and across the flat. No skate/pole detail could be confirmed because the footage is a POV glide.
 - Video 6 t=180: the sign at the fork: the black "Chute" arrow up-left, and "Grandfathers Trail" green going left toward the village. So Chute and Grandfathers sit at one fork very close to the top; Chute drops off the lip to the skier's left into steeper open/glade terrain with small rimed trees at the lip.
 - Video 5 t=87-95: Quickdraw top-side: a lift building on the right, a snow fence line on the left, young pines; low drift. Grandfathers Trail lower down (video 6 t=245-265) is a gentle wide groom with trees on both sides.
 - I did not get footage of Gunbarrel, Twenty Two, Peashooter, Gromit, K, K2, Magnum, Face or Poma by name.

2. The run under the chair, The Pit, Grouse Gulch
 - Not found in this lap (no labelled footage reached). Only the generic lower-mountain groom (video 6 t=430-465) was seen: wide, gentle, spruce on the left.

3. T-Bar freestyle area (the mogul hill)
 - Video 1 t=80-100: the NORAM mogul course: lane about 3-4 skiers wide; a blue fence on the skier's right along the whole lane; red/orange padded rectangular markers inside the course edges; officials and spectators cluster at the top left. Tall green snow-laden trees line both sides closely. The bumps are medium and regular; pitch moderate.
 - Video 1 t=75: viewed from the course, a yellow lodge/cabin building and a second cabin sit down-left toward the village, with a small orange block at the lip. Guess: this is the freestyle cabin and the kids' area.
 - Video 1 t=150: yellow padded T-bar towers.
 - Video 6 t=1460-1470: a wide shot from the top: village below, a blue fence line at the bottom left of the frame, a cat track on the right, and forest. This puts the mogul area on the village side of the T-Bar with forest on its right side.
 - Old Mill (video 2): a wide groom on the right of the T-Bar, with green conifers along its left edge, which is consistent with "trees between the mogul area and Old Mill".
 - Not seen: the two jumps explicitly, a judges' stand, or a finish-area building (only the lodge-like building on the left).

4. Bowl runs and the Wild Side
 - Video 4 shows the Grandmother's Trail start: a signed shoulder with a wooden fence line along the lip, an open slope, very sparse small trees, and views of a big dark ridge. Spring crust.
 - Video 5 t=120-150: open snowfield "off the top", moderate pitch, a few small green trees, powder. Not named.
 - Video 3 (Ridge Run): the open rolling shoulder at the top.

5. Wide shots showing several runs
 - Video 6 t=1460-1470 (best), video 3 t=18-35 (far ridge), video 4 t=35-75 (ridge).
