# Batch 3: The Pit, West Bank, K2, slack country
- The Pit: a narrow traverse drops into a steep bowl-like fall-line cut. Chopped powder with small-to-medium moguls, ungroomed. Snow ghosts at the top give way to dense dark-green trees at the edges. The Quickdraw cables run overhead. Blue-sky high contrast. "That snow is fabulous."
- West Bank: steep and sustained, narrower and more chute-like, moguled, with tight tree walls. A lift tower with a yellow padded base sits right on the skiable edge.
- K2: very steep with a blind rollover at the top, then wider and more open (dense trees on the left, opening up on the right), moguled. Green snowy trees, not rimed. Overcast. A small hut and a "BEST WAY DOWN" sign near the run-out.
- Slack country: a flat cat track looking out at avalanche slide paths, then a sharp exposed ridge with sparse snow ghosts. "We got the Tooth and the Tusk ... kind of gnarly lines." Monochrome, overcast.
- Extra: the moguls run out onto a fast groomer. Hot-sauce shots on the chair (the chapter called "ski sauce").
- Dev takeaways: chair cables as a leading line over The Pit; padded towers close to the run edges; a blind rollover entry on K2; a jagged Tooth and Tusk ridge landmark; slide paths visible beyond the boundary.
