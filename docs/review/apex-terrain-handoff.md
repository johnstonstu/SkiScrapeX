## Summary

- **Inputs:** 45 public YouTube POV videos identified (64 run segments; Rise & Alpine chapter video `An8kSTvZv_k`, Adam Romagnoli and Oviatt series, others with chapters or timestamps). I downloaded 480p segments with yt-dlp; 39 videos produced usable segments. I pulled 342 stills with ffmpeg (57 run segments × 6) and reviewed the montages by eye. I combined that with the LidarBC 2025 1 m DTM/DSM (canopy = DSM − DTM, trees = >2.5 m), OSM pistes, the game's `features.json` / `runOverlay.ts` / `corrections.ts` / `park.ts` (read-only), the official grooming run list and the 2025/26 trail map plus its Wildside inset. The trail map was used for placement only and is not redistributed.
- **run-profiles.json:** 90 records (75 official OSM lines or line parts, including the proposed splits; 11 drafted run lines; 4 glade polygons; parks included). Each record has a 20 m slope profile, steep sections, pitch breaks, open width, edge tree density and canopy height, plus the surface and moguls seen in frames, observation notes with video and timestamp, and difficulty corrections.
- **19 unmapped runs:** 15 got draft geometry (11 lines + 4 glade polygons), all marked `approximate: true` with a confidence level. Little Nuggets is already in the game (`park.ts`, way/1105323712). Gauntlet, Glade-iator and Panhandler are **unplaced**: no map label and no footage, so they are null-geometry placeholders.
  - New lines (6): Toilet Bowl (low-medium), Essendale Ward 1 (low), Essendale Ward 2 (low), Tooth Tusk (low-medium), Sweet Sue (low), Wildly Easy (medium).
  - Renamed existing game traces (5, geometry unchanged): W3→Jackpot, W4→Gun Slinger, W5→ShootOut, W6→Showdown (all medium-low), L3→Chaser (low).
  - Glade polygons (4, snapped to LiDAR canopy-cover gaps): Any Which Way Glades (low-medium), Granny's Glades (low), Fools Gold (low-medium), Hidden Gold (low).
- **Splits/merge:** Gambit at 582 m (medium), Spruce Hollow at 244 m (low-medium), Hang 'em High at 1140 m (medium-low), and Wishbone merged into one blue run (medium).
- **Forest:** 5 m canopy cover and height PNGs over the LiDAR extent (wider than the terrain grid), a drop-in 60 m patch for `horizon-canopy.u8`, and forest-stand polygons for the ring outside the terrain grid. Calibration finding: in the overlap, the MRDEM-based `apron.ts` cover is already **higher** than the LiDAR measurement (0.76 vs 0.49 mean), so sparse distant forest is more likely a tree instance density/LOD issue than missing canopy data **[inferred]**.

## Video analysis

A full watch of Rise & Alpine's Apex video (YouTube `An8kSTvZv_k`) is in [apex-video/ANALYSIS.md](apex-video/ANALYSIS.md), with chapter timings in `apex-video/chapters.json` and batch notes in `apex-video/notes/`. Same status as the rest of this handoff: a proposal to review, not an applied change.

- **Altitude tree biome:** ordinary snowy conifers lower down, rime-encased snow ghosts near the summit and ridges, spaced wider up top.
- **Atmosphere:** summit fog that clears lower down, plus a valley cloud inversion seen from Ridge Run and the groomers.
- **Audio by surface:** zipper and carve plus wind on groomers, scrape and chop on blacks, rhythmic thuds in moguls, a muffled swoosh in powder, rock scrape on thin cover.
- **Traffic bumps:** irregular bumps under the lifts.
- **Landmarks:** summit lattice radio tower, orange slat fence, Tooth/Tusk wooden signpost, and padded lift towers close to the run edges.
- **Shoot the Chute:** a gentle approach, then a blind rollover. A speed trap just past that rollover is the natural max-speed moment. Finish at the upper parking lot run-out (from Stu). The video does not show the run-out, so use the LiDAR profile for it.

Corrections from the full watch: the chapter titled "gromit" is Mini Colorado (on-screen label). The game only has it as placeholder L12, so this is reference footage for that trace. The "mogul team Alberta" chapter has no mogul course, only kids at the T-bar base.

## Confidence and caveats

- **APPROXIMATE geometry:** the south-face lines come from trail-map *order* (Sweet Sue, Tooth/Tusk, Essendale, Toilet Bowl, Crusher, left to right) projected onto LiDAR fall lines. The art is not to scale, so the exact lines may be off by one or two gullies. Wildly Easy is a least-cost path. The Wildside names rely on inset order matching the existing traces. Chaser (L3) is a guess from neighbours and pitch.
- **Glade polygons:** extents come from canopy cover thresholds inside a corridor guessed from the map. The boundaries along the slope are the weakest part.
- **Observations** come from 3–6 sampled frames per segment. "Not seen" does not mean absent. Items marked **[inferred]** in notes are interpretations. Frame dates vary by season and year, so grooming and moguls change day to day.
- **LiDAR** was flown in July 2025 (summer), so open widths and canopy reflect summer ground, and snow will narrow edges. Slopes are 30 m smoothed.
- **Essendale Ward 1/2:** the R&A chapter just says "Essendale". The split into two lines is from the grooming list, not footage.
- **Difficulty:** the official colours come from the grooming list and maps. The game's `novice` vs `easy` semantics are unconfirmed.
- **Frame grabs** are low-res stills from public YouTube videos, kept for reference only. Don't ship them as game assets. The trail maps are not included in the bundle.
- **CC photos** are NC-SA/NC-ND, so they're fine for internal reference but unsuitable for a commercial or derived game asset.
