# Apex Mountain Terrain Park History by Season

Compiled 2026-11-24 from `apexresort.com` (live & Wayback Machine archives 2007–2025), Newschoolers reports (2005–2006), Penticton Western News (2011, 2017), SkiCanada.org, Castanet/Apex Matters (2025–2026), and `/workspace/apex-media/index.csv` + `_game/features.json`.

## Evolution Summary

Apex's terrain park footprint evolved across four distinct eras:
1. **2005–2011 (World Cup & Dual-Park Era):** Orage Terrain Park (comp park), Crooked Park on Maverick (intermediate/all-level rail & box park), and the 2005–2008 Super-Pipe excavated in dirt on Okanagan Run.
2. **2011–2015 (Claim Jumper & Relocated Progression Era):** Orage rebranded to Claim Jumper (by March 2011); Maverick's Crooked Park became Maverick Park and was moved in 2014–15 to skier's left of Claim Jumper and renamed **Prospector**; a dedicated **Night Park** launched on Okanagan Run; and a permanent **Skier/Boarder Cross** track opened skier's right off the T-Bar above Spruce Hollow.
3. **2016–2018 (Rockstar Sponsorship & Magic Carpet Era):** Rockstar Energy sponsored Claim Jumper (10–50 ft tables, hips, 50-ft jump, 30+ rails); a beginner **Magic Carpet Terrain Park** launched on the Bunny Hill; Night Park operated Fri/Sat 4–9 pm ($7.50 Freaky Friday).
4. **2019–2026 (Four-Park Standardized Progression):** Little Nuggets (Magic Carpet), Okanagan Terrain Park (T-Bar + Fri/Sat night-lit), Prospector (T-Bar progression, up to 20 ft), and Claim Jumper (T-Bar big features, 20–50 ft).

## Season-by-Season Layouts

### 2005–2006
- **Super-Pipe (Okanagan Run):** Built in dirt in 2005 to full height; bottom excavated to 16 degrees; dedicated tower-mounted snowgun; light tower moved for grinder access; dedicated snowcat with pipe grinder. Hosted Feb 2006 local Halfpipe contest & March 2006 FIS Freestyle World Cup Finals (won by Sarah Burke & Kalle Leinonen).
- **Rail Park & Air Site:** Newschoolers reports (Dec 2005, Feb 2006) describe a modest public rail park ("short boxes, a couple of rails"), a summer dirt-built jump zone, and an **Aerial Site** restricted to freestyle club athletes (also used for Sunday Big Air contest).
- **Sources:** `https://www.newschoolers.com/threads/apex-park.179288/`, `https://www.newschoolers.com/threads/how-is-apex-mountain-resort.195443/`, `https://www.newschoolers.com/threads/apex-girl-crew.256712/`

### 2006–2007 to 2010–2011
- **Orage Terrain Park:** Competition-oriented layout; line of large jumps on skier's right, big rail line on skier's left; upgraded snowmaking pumps/guns for earlier opening.
- **Crooked Park (Maverick):** Laid-back park on Maverick (Stock's Chair, `1279 m`, `13.1 deg` avg) for all riding levels. Named features from 2007–2011 Wayback pages:
  - C-Boxes (configured as an X-Box)
  - 20-ft Big Box
  - "Moby" feature
  - S-Rail
  - Flat-Down rail
  - "R3" feature
- **Super-Pipe (Okanagan Run):** Listed on 2007, 2008, and 2009 park pages (hosted 2007 FIS Freestyle World Cup Halfpipe, Feb 23, 2007); dropped from the 2011 park page.
- **Sources:** Wayback `20070104` / `20080106` / `20090101` / `20110210` snapshots of `http://www.apexresort.com/mountain/parks.php`

### 2011–2012 to 2013–2014
- **Claim Jumper Terrain Park:** Rebranded from Orage by March 2011 (hosted 2011 Shred Stock Slopestyle). Upgraded tables with step-ups and multiple takeoffs (`SkiCanada.org`), 10–50 ft tables, hips, 30+ rails.
- **Village Rail Jam Setup (2011):** Temporary event setup near Gunbarrel Saloon featuring a hand-built 20-ft round down bar and roller coaster bars (Freeride Boardshop).
- **Maverick Park:** Continuation of Crooked Park on Maverick prior to its 2014–15 relocation.
- **Skier / Boardercross Track:** Built with banked turns featuring up to 5 m (15 ft) walls (`SkiCanada.org`), located skier's right off the T-Bar above Spruce Hollow.
- **Sources:** `https://pentictonwesternnews.com/2011/03/29/skiers-and-snowboarders-grind-rails-at-apex/`, `https://www.skicanada.org/profile-bcs-apex-mountain-resorts-olympic-ski-style/`

### 2014–2015
- **Claim Jumper Park (T-Bar, `570 m`, `154 m` vert):** L & XL features, 10–50 ft tables, transfers, flat-downs, C-boxes, S-rails, rainbows, tree bonks, 30+ rails.
- **Prospector (New Name!):** Moved Maverick Park over to skier's left of Claim Jumper (`210 m`, `52 m` vert in OSM `way/374339370`) as the beginner/progression rail & box park.
- **Night Park (Okanagan Run):** Fully lit Friday & Saturday nights; mix of rails, boxes, small and large jumps.
- **Skier and Snowboard Cross:** "New and improved" family/racing track located skier's right off the T-Bar above Spruce Hollow.
- **Sources:** Wayback `20150111` `http://www.apexresort.com/the-mountain/terrain-parks/`

### 2016–2018
- **Rockstar Claim Jumper Park:** Sponsored by Rockstar Energy; L & XL features, 10–50 ft tables (including 50-ft feature jump), hips, flat-downs, C-boxes, 30+ rails, Rockstar rail. Hosted Jan 29, 2017 Pentagon Rail Jam.
- **Prospector Park:** Intermediate progression park, skier's left of Claim Jumper.
- **Night Park – Okanagan Run:** Lit Fri/Sat 4–9 pm ($7.50 Freaky Friday pass); jumps, rails, boxes.
- **Magic Carpet Terrain Park:** New first-timer park on the Bunny Hill / Easy Rider (`196 m`, `7.3 deg`).
- **Skier and Snowboard Cross:** Maintained above Spruce Hollow.
- **Sources:** Wayback `20170129` `https://www.apexresort.com/the-mountain/terrain-parks/`

### 2019–2026 (Current 4-Park Progression)
- **Little Nuggets Terrain Park (Magic Carpet):** Renamed from Magic Carpet Park. Located right side of Magic Carpet; couple 5-ft roll-overs, couple boxes, small rail. (Not mapped in OSM.)
- **Okanagan Terrain Park (T-Bar, `89 m` OSM `way/1105323712`, `lit=yes`):** Renamed from Night Park. Adjacent to T-Bar; only park open at night Fri & Sat. Features 5–15 ft roll-overs, larger/more difficult rails, selection of jumps, plus cannon feature at bottom (2026 ElleBoard/GROM report). Hosted 2024–2026 Okanagan Shred Sessions Slopestyle.
- **Prospector Terrain Park (T-Bar, `210 m` OSM `way/374339370`, `13.8 deg` avg):** Full-size progression park with skill-building jumps & roll-overs up to 20 ft, boxes, rails, small gaps.
- **Claim Jumper Terrain Park (T-Bar, `570 m` OSM `way/374339381`, `15.1 deg` avg):** Advanced big-feature park with 20–50 ft jumps, challenging step-ups, larger jump gaps, longer rails. *(Note: in 2023–24, Prospector and Claim Jumper had intermittent maintenance/snow-year closures while Okanagan stayed open.)*
- **Note on Game `features.json`:** `terrainParks` currently has 5 entries: `Claim Jumper`, `Boarder Cross` (above Spruce Hollow), `Park` (Okanagan Park, `lit=yes`), and two unnamed `None` snow_park lines near Claim Jumper/Prospector (`way/374339369` & `way/374339370`). `Little Nuggets` is unmapped in OSM.
- **Sources:** `https://apexresort.com/terrain-parks/`, Wayback `20190107`/`20200309`/`20220228`/`20240225`, `https://cdnc.heyzine.com/flip-book/pdf/b44f68c6ac868ab6f0b18d8b1a10f409472e6c7f.pdf`
