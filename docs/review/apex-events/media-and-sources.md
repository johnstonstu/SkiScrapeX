## Notable Athletes & Coaches Connected to Apex (Event Context)
- **Kristi Richards** (Summerland / Apex Freestyle Club; 2x Olympian, 2007 World Champion, 2007 Apex World Cup bronze; **Kristi's Run** named in her honour; AFC Head Coach 2020)
- **Andi Naude** (Penticton / AFC; 2014 Canadian Mogul Champion, Olympian; trained & cheered at Apex NorAm)
- **Reece Howden** (Apex Ski Club alum; raced 2015 Teck Slalom on Okanagan Run; World Cup & World Champion Ski Cross)
- **Josh Dueck** (arrived 1999 for moguls, AFC head coach 2002; Paralympic gold/silver & X Games medalist; first sit-ski backflip on snow)
- **Eric Pehota & Trevor Petersen** (arrived 1982 as liftie/ski tech; raced Shoot the Chute at 151 km/h before pioneering Canadian big-mountain ski mountaineering)
- **David Boehm** (founded Apex Freestyle Club Dec 1990), **Wade Garrod** (coached 1994–2007), **Rob Kober** (20-yr national moguls coach & AFC head coach), **Kenni Kuroda**, **Alexa Chick**, **Jorgen Anderson** (Apex Ski Club head coach).

## Explicit Gaps & Uncertainties
1. **Shoot the Chute exact years & timing setup:** Verified via Eric Pehota's 2025 Mountain Life interview (151 km/h on 225 cm Atomics; Petersen in Stanfield underwear), and Chute is confirmed as one of Apex's original 1961 runs ending at the old lodge/upper lot. Exact decade span (likely 1980s), official start/finish gate markers, and timing/radar hardware are not documented online.
2. **Mars Downhill ("Mars Bar") exact run segments & 2005 J1 Nationals site:** Verified as **Mars Canadian Junior Championships (Jan 2004 at Apex)** and **Mars BC Cup Apex Speed Series (Feb 2005 at Apex)**, plus FIS speed homologations `4810-321-97`, `7414-001-04`, and `9460/01/10` ("Gunbarrel" downhill track, 2175->1643 m, 2,002 m, 532 m drop, 36 gates). Exact turn-by-turn run junctions (e.g., Ridge Run -> Gunbarrel -> Wild Bill vs Old Mill) are not spelled out in the FIS results tables; also Pique's March 2005 J1 Nationals article didn't state which resort hosted the March 2005 J1 finals (vs February's Apex Speed Series).
3. **Terrain Park maps & build videos:** Specific seasonal park feature maps (PDFs/JPGs from 2006–2023) and dedicated Apex park-crew build edits are sparse online (many older Flash/gallery links on Wayback `apexresort.com` did not archive).
4. **Ski/Boardercross competition results:** The permanent track above Spruce Hollow is well-documented (2013–2018), including in `features.json`, but specific regional SBX/SX race dates/results at Apex were not found in public archives.
5. **1990–2002 early AFC moguls & 1960s–1980s club race records:** Mostly offline in club/Penticton archives.
6. **Pond Skim / Slush Cup & Uphill:** Apex does not appear to run a Pond Skim (Slush Cup is Baldy Mountain); uphill travel is prohibited inside Apex's Controlled Recreation Area (`The Good, The Bad, The Ugly` skin track goes to secondary saddle outside/edge).
